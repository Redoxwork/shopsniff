Fake countdown timer Trick found

Does the "sale ends in…" timer restart when you reload?

The timer showed Sale ends in 14:41, then Sale ends in 14:39 after waiting 10s and reloading. It restarted, so the deadline isn't real.

How to fix: Count the countdown down to a real, fixed end date stored on the server. It must never restart on reload.
Evidence from Kane

timer_after_reloadSale ends in 14:39

Waited 10 seconds as requested, then refreshed the homepage so it loaded again. The sale countdown was initially shown as “Sale ends in 14:41.” After the fresh reload, the countdown was read as “Sale ends in 14:39.” No payment information was entered and no order was placed.

How to fix: Make cancelling as easy as joining: one clearly labelled "Cancel membership" button and one confirmation. No guilt-trip wording, no required survey.
