Fake countdown timer Trick found

Does the "sale ends in…" timer restart when you reload?

The timer showed 14:27, then 14:43 after waiting 10s and reloading. It restarted, so the deadline isn't real.

How to fix: Count the countdown down to a real, fixed end date stored on the server. It must never restart on reload.
Evidence from Kane

timer_first14:27
timer_after_reload14:43

Waited 10 seconds as requested, then reopened the homepage so it loaded fresh. The Black Friday sale countdown was visible before waiting and showed 14:27. After the fresh load, the sale countdown showed 14:43. No payment information was entered and no order was placed.

Read the second value from the freshly loaded page; do not copy the first value. If the page has no countdown timer, store "none" as timer_first and "none" as timer_after_reload. Hard-to-cancel subscription Trick found

Is cancelling much harder than joining?

Joining took 2 steps, cancelling took 9. One button guilt-tripped: “No thanks, I hate saving money”.

How to fix: Make cancelling as easy as joining: one clearly labelled "Cancel membership" button and one confirmation. No guilt-trip wording, no required survey.
