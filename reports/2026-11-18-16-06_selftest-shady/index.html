The timer showed Sale ends in 14:45, then 14:52 after waiting 10s and reloading. It restarted, so the deadline isn't real.

How to fix: Count the countdown down to a real, fixed end date stored on the server. It must never restart on reload.

Hard-to-cancel subscription Trick found

Is cancelling much harder than joining?

Joining took 2 steps, cancelling took 11. One button guilt-tripped: “No thanks, I hate saving money”.

How to fix: Make cancelling as easy as joining: one clearly labelled "Cancel membership" button and one confirmation. No guilt-trip wording, no required survey.
